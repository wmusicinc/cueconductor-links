    Run a cue from the podium¶

    Everything a conductor touches during a take, and nothing else.

    The screen¶

    • Counter: bar, beat dots and meter, over the scoring video.
    • Streamers travel across the picture and land on the beat. A courtesy meter trails the first bar of a new time signature.
    • Transport along the bottom: step back a bar, Play, Stop, step forward a bar, with the time and timecode readout.
    • Click and FOH faders at the sides, each with pan, mute and a meter.
    • Stem mixer: open the drawer to see every stem with its own fader, solo and mute.

    Transport¶

    • Play starts from the current position after the cue's own pre-roll clicks.
    • Stop parks where you are. Stop twice quickly returns to the start of the cue.
    • Step buttons move one bar at a time while parked.
    • Drag in the scrub bar under the picture to locate anywhere. The filmstrip above it shows where you are in the video.

    Locate¶

    • Type a bar number and press Go. With playback stopped you land there parked; with playback running you land there playing.
    • The marker list has every rehearsal letter and section name in the cue. Jump & Pause sets a section up; Jump & Play rolls straight into it.

    Mix without leaving the podium¶

    Open the stem drawer. Each stem has a fader, solo and mute. Solo across several stems is a mode, and the master solo button blinks while it is on so you never leave it armed by accident. Restore Defaults returns the mix the cue's author set.

    Pre-roll¶

    The cue's author set how many prep clicks precede the first bar. The counter shows PRE-ROLL and counts them down so you can bring the orchestra in on the downbeat.

    If it doesn't work¶

    • Play does nothing. Check that the device is not locked to a master in Sync Settings; a following device does not start on its own.
    • The picture is black after the last frame. That is the video-less tail of the cue; the counter keeps running to the end bar.
    • The click is late in Bluetooth earbuds. See Earbuds on a stand.
