    Conduct the room¶

    One podium, every stand. Switch on Conduct and every CueConductor on the Wi-Fi follows: counter, beat dots, streamers and page turns, each player reading their own part. No cables, no MIDI, no audio to distribute.

    On the podium¶

    1. Open the cue.
    2. Tap Sync and switch on Conduct (Broadcast). The sheet reports when it is searching for stands and when they join.
    3. Optionally switch on Push Cues to Stands. Every cue you open is then sent in the background to stands that do not have it: MIDI map, overlay, score and parts and a mono click, no stems or video. A stand that already has the full cue keeps its own.
    4. Press Play. The room follows.

    External Sync input is unavailable while conducting; a podium either leads or follows.

    On each stand¶

    1. Open the same cue, or any cue if the podium pushes cues.
    2. Tap Sync, choose Link to DAW, and leave Follow Master on Any Master.
    3. Switch on Follow Master's Cue so the stand opens whatever cue the podium opens, and Accept Pushed Cues if the podium pushes.

    The full player-side steps are on Set up a stand.

    Several rooms on one network¶

    With two podiums or a podium and a DAW on the same Wi-Fi, each stand picks its master under Follow Master. Masters appear in the list once they have tried to connect. Give your device a name under Device Display Name so players can tell rooms apart.

    USB instead of Wi-Fi¶

    A Mac and an iPad connected by cable form a network of their own. Everything above works with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off on both.

    If it doesn't work¶

    • A stand says waiting to connect. Both devices must be on the same network and the podium must have Conduct on. Corporate and guest Wi-Fi often block device-to-device traffic; a hotspot or a travel router solves it.
    • The stand opens the wrong cue. Follow Master's Cue applies when the transport stops, never mid-playback. Stop and it switches.
    • A pushed cue has no sound but click. That is by design. Pushed copies show a Pushed badge in the library and carry no stems or video.
